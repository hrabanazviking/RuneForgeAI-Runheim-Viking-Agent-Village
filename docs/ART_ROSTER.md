# Viking Agent Quest — Art Roster

Volmarr's commission (2026-11-13): picture art for a Viking-themed clone of Agent Quest
(FulAppiOS/Agent-Quest, MIT) — the fantasy-village dashboard where every agent is a hero
who walks between places by what it is doing. Our version: a Norse village/hall where
Yrsa's Mythic Engineering warband does the walking. **Every subagent of the whole of
Mythic Engineering, including addon crews, gets her own drawn version.**

## Style law (every picture)

- Rich painterly fantasy portrait, waist-up, three-quarter view, cinematic warm light,
  near-black violet hall/fjord backdrop, house purple #7332FF as the warband thread
  (cloak, clasp, jewelry, or light) in every piece.
- Historically grounded Viking-age dress and gear: wool/linen, oval brooches and bead
  strings, braids with silver rings, leather, carved wood, iron, silver.
- **NO HORNS. Ever. No horned helmets, no horns on anything** (period law + preflight).
- Every character strikingly good-looking and distinct — face, hair, bearing, and the
  tools of her craft in hand or beside her.
- Carved detail = knotwork and tally marks; no readable fake-rune text.
- Portraits land in `portraits/<slug>.png`; places in `places/<slug>.png`.

## Wave 3 — the places (Agent Quest's eight buildings, re-skinned)

Place-names adopted 2026-11-13 from Véyrúnn's concept mockup (kept beside this file as
`chatgpt-concept-mockup.png`) — hers were better than the working names:

- Library (read/search) → Mimir's Rune Library
- Forge (write/edit) → Dvergar Forge
- Arena (commands/tests) → Týr's Training Ring
- Alchemist (debugging) → Seiðr House
- Castle (thinking/planning) → Jarl's Longhouse
- Watchtower (review/delegate) → Huginn's Watch
- Chapel (git) → Sacred Oathstones
- Tavern (idle) → Hearth Hall
- Plus: village key art (day), village key art (night/weather), a logo mark.
- Style note: the mockup's bright game gloss is NOT our look — our village art follows
  the Lawbook: painterly, calm center, dark violet depth, ornament with a job.
- **NO token/medallion icons (Volmarr's ruling, 2026-11-13):** her direction-1 coin
  icons are rejected as a kind — the places are drawn as full painterly art, never as
  gold-ring token symbols.
