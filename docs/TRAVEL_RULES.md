# Village Travel Rules — how heroes move in the Viking Agent Village

*Set by Yrsa at Volmarr's word, 2026-11-13: "make good rules for how the characters travel around." These govern the mirror map now and the local live hall when it is built.*

1. **Roads, not crow-flight.** Heroes travel along the village's paths between places — a waypoint graph over the painted ground — never in straight lines through buildings, water, or the middle of the oathstone ring.
2. **Never through anyone.** Two heroes on one path keep a polite offset and pass without overlapping; a hero never walks through another hero, a place's doorway crowd, or a resting group.
3. **Pace tells the state.** Working heroes walk briskly; thinking heroes stroll slowly; a hero carrying fresh word (new spawn, just-banked result) hurries; waiting heroes stand easy at their place; resting heroes do not wander at all — they sit in the Hearth Hall.
4. **Arrivals come by the shore.** A newly spawned hero walks in from the dock at the village edge to their first place. A hero who leaves the word walks to the dock and fades at the water — nobody pops in or out of existence.
5. **Finished work is sworn first.** A hero whose task completes walks to the Sacred Oathstones (the banking) before going anywhere else; only then do they walk to rest or to their next place.
6. **No teleporting, ever.** If a hero's place changed while the map was hidden or stale, they still walk it when seen again — the pace scales with distance so no walk runs longer than ~6 seconds, but the feet always make the journey.
7. **Families travel together.** When a coordinator and their children change place in the same snapshot, the children follow in loose file behind the coordinator rather than scattering.
8. **Night settles the village.** When the snapshot's word grows old and the night painting comes, heroes finish the walk they are on and settle at their places; no new strolls begin until fresh word arrives.
9. **The jarl's call has right of way.** A hero whose state is *waiting on Volmarr* walks to the Jarl's Longhouse by the straightest road at hurry pace, drawn above the crowd, with their need shown — the village's version of the needs-you queue.
10. **Idle strolling stays home.** A hero at their place may drift in a small loop around it (a few steps, then a pause) — theatre, not travel; the stroll never crosses into another place's ground, and the hero's *place* is always the truth the snapshot spoke.
